Detailed Description

How the synthetic sensor turns starlight into 8-bit pixel values.

Levels are in counts (8-bit digital numbers).

◆ zeroPoint

decimal scope::SensorModel::zeroPoint = DECIMAL(50000.0)

Total counts from a magnitude 0 star; magnitude m gives zeroPoint * 10^(-0.4 m).

The default puts the detection limit for –centroid-threshold 40 near magnitude 5.5 and saturates stars brighter than about 3.5.
