Evaluates the Jacobian of the measurement model (paper Eq.

15) for one star.

The attitude columns are for a correction applied as AttitudeCorrection(dtheta) * attitude, which moves the camera-frame direction by e_C x dtheta to first order (Eq. 35).

Parameters
eCThe star's unit line of sight in the camera frame, with the current attitude applied. Must have z > 0.
cameraThe camera parameters to evaluate at.
Returns
The partial derivatives of [u', v'].
