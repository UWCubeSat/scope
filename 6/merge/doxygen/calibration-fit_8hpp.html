Fits the camera parameters and attitude corrections to star centroids with the paper's Levenberg-Marquardt method (Eqs.

20-37), refining the attitude of image k as AttitudeCorrection(dtheta_k) * prior_k (Eq. 11). Outliers are rejected by refitting without them, which the paper does not do.

An image with fewer than three inliers (one under the shared model) is left out, and gets the mean correction of the others applied to its prior.

The result is not converged if the last fit hit maxIterations or stalled, or if too few observations were left to fit; with none fitted at all, it holds the prior.

Parameters
observationsThe star-centroid stage's output.
priorThe prior camera parameters, where the fit starts.
settingsWhich parameters to estimate and how.
Returns
The fitted calibration.
Exceptions
std::invalid_argumentif an observation refers to an image with no attitude.
