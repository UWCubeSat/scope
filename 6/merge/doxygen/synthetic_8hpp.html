Detailed Description

Synthetic star fields from a known camera and known attitudes, for testing the pipeline end to end.

Everything here goes through ProjectStarToPixel, so it shares the camera model with stages 2 and 3. What it checks independently is the rest: the dark frame, the pixel convention, centroiding, matching, and the fit.
